Good questions to type

To Hunter (Opportunities page):

  • Why does the Field meals for Camp Mackall match me?
  • Compare my top 3: which should I chase?
  • What's new since last week?
  • Show only woman-owned set-asides. / Only federal. / Under $500K.
  • Find work in Virginia. / What is a sources sought?

To Grant (any opportunity page, right-hand panel):

  • Can I fix this in time? · Where am I weak? · What should my response say? · Do I have enough time?

Any other question gets a sensible answer and buttons for the nearest questions. Nothing dead-ends.

Before and between calls

  • Click "Reset demo" (top right) before each call. It restores the starting story: wallet $306, nothing extra booked or sent.
  • The demo remembers what you do during a call, even after a reload.
  • The thin strip at the top says the data is sample data about a fictional business. Keep it: it is honest, and prospects accept it.
  • Dates move with the calendar. Deadlines never go stale.
  • Ava's hidden controls: Shift + Alt + A on an Ava bid lets you jump to any of Ava's states.
  • Presenter panel: Shift + Alt + P, or the faint handle at the bottom of the menu. Sarah answers a waiting review, a sent bid gets its award, and 1–9, 0, − jump to any segment of the story. Booth mode returns to Hunter's feed after 3 minutes with nobody at the laptop.
  • Use a laptop browser, 1280 px wide or more.

Say it carefully

  • Don't promise wins. Hunter finds and explains; Grant advises; the member decides.
  • Mentor reviews take about one business day in the product. Say so when you use Sarah answers: "She answers within a business day, so let's jump ahead a day."
